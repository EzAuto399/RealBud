# Portal explore

Bud uses its work browser like a careful new staff member: it looks around the
portal the person is already signed in to, finds the way to what was asked,
and writes the way down only when the person agrees.

## When to use

- The person asks "find out how to export the tenant list from REI", "where is
  the supplier list in REI", or a refresh says the export's place in the
  portal is not mapped yet.
- Only inside a browser task the person started in Ask. Without one, ask them
  to start it.

## How to explore

1. Borrow the portal tab and read the page before every step.
2. Open menus, lists, report lists and tabs, and choose view or output options
   such as "Export Only". These only show things. The portal's own menu and
   report names usually go through without a card; anything else asks the
   person once.
3. Never press a control that changes records or reaches people: Email, SMS,
   Letter, Send, Generate, Import, Process, Save, Post, Delete, Finalise,
   Reconcile, Pay, Notice or anything like them. If the only way forward is
   one of those, stop and tell the person what you found.
4. Never type into sign-in, password or code fields. If the portal asks you to
   sign in, stop and let the person sign in themselves, then carry on.
5. A download asks the person every time. One download is enough to confirm
   the export works; do not repeat it.
6. Stay out of Settings and other areas the portal map marks as off limits.

## Proposing the path

When you have reached the export, use the browser's propose-path tool once
with the recipe slot (for example the tenant list or the supplier list) and
the steps in order: the menu or page you opened, each control you used, the
option you chose and the final download.

- Use only the exact control names you actually used in this task. A step you
  did not take, or a control that changes records, is refused.
- The person sees the steps on a card and decides. Until they allow it,
  nothing is saved. Say plainly that the refresh will use the new path and
  still asks before each download.

## Reply

Tell the person in two or three sentences where the thing lives, what you
proposed, and that nothing in the portal was changed.
