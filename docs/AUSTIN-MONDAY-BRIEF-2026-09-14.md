## The offer in ordinary words

“RealBud gives your office an assistant called Bud. It works through the tasks we agree, using your emails, appointments and files, and saves work for Kevin to check. We begin with his morning priorities, missing bills and unclear ANZ references. Once the foundation is in place, other departments can reuse it. You pay for the additional work without paying for the same core setup again.”

The initial scope remains Kevin, one agreed Windows computer, up to two approved Gmail accounts, one calendar, one Drive source tree, one output folder, one ANZ/REI format and one phone route. Included Google work, five templates, weekly summary and guide retain their controls. The 1,500+ catalogue offers future connection choices, not 1,500 configured workflows. No outgoing messages, money movement, signing or legal notices. Airbnb/Form 11 remain excluded.

## Present in 15 minutes

1. **Pages 1-2, 5 minutes:** morning priorities, expected bills and unclear bank references. Let Kevin explain what the examples would help with and what he still needs to check.
2. **Page 3, 2 minutes:** included Bud and Google Workspace work. Show the complete task: check agreed emails/appointments, find relevant files, save a linked priority list. Explain the later connection choices without engine jargon.
3. **Page 4, 3 minutes:** A$4,500 total, A$1,500 deposit to start. Kickoff checks and planning are included. Three A$1,000 milestones follow. Two care months are included; A$299/month starts in month three. The initial A$75 usage allowance is reviewed during the first 30 days after handover/go-live, with any future change agreed in writing. No automatic increase or retrospective charge. Setup and care reference prices are our estimates, not verified past sales.
4. **Pages 5-6, 3 minutes:** target 2-3 weeks build/test once plan/access are ready, one teaching week, then one month observation after accepted go-live. Later departments reuse the core but have separate scope/quotes. CRM is A$1,500 setup and A$149 monthly care. Local Mac: A$1,500 hardware budget + A$450 provisioning and about A$15/month backup. Cloud: about A$60/month hosting/backup, with deployment inside CRM setup. CRM-inclusive totals are about A$164 or A$209; add A$299 Bud care when payable. Show budgets as estimates pending exact selection. Both can host CRM; a cloud CRM server does not operate Kevin's computer.
5. **Close, 2 minutes:** agree kickoff and resolve the review agreement. Signing page 17 orders the A$4,500 project with its A$1,500 deposit. Schedule A confirms the implementation plan before build, not a second purchase. Optional services need their own orders. FAQ is page 7.

Use plain office language and labelled illustrations. Explain that Bud prepares and Kevin controls the business decision. Do not claim quantified savings or that every added department will be cheaper regardless of complexity. The credible benefit is reuse: no repeat core charge, only incremental work. Hardware budget includes GST; service fees add GST where applicable. Confirm provider costs, licence fees, hardware stock/capacity, start dates and backup/recovery ownership before an optional order.
