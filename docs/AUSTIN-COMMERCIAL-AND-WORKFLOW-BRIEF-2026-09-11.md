## The workflow contract we deliver

Every routine is supplied as a versioned, inactive configuration. ### P1 · Payment-reference preparation

- **Trigger:** daily or an explicitly anchored two-day interval; confirm weekends, Brisbane timezone and source date coverage. Do not treat every two days as simply Monday/Wednesday/Friday.
- **Inputs:** authorised bank/account/export route, accepted columns and date/amount format, approved payer/property/reference directory, REI preview rules. One bank/REI layout is included.
- **Execution:** verify account and requested date range → download complete source → preserve bytes/digest and coverage → identify previously reviewed exports and possible overlaps → propose approved reference changes → hold unclear/duplicate/existing-reference rows → review all rows → export a reference-only copy → inspect REI preview and totals → authorised staff confirms the final result.
- **Human authority:** no amount-only property guesses; retain ambiguous and genuinely identical transactions. Review permission does not grant posting, payment or bank-detail changes. REI is the final financial system.
- **Recovery:** login/MFA releases control and saves the hold. Allow at most two additional transient read/download retries within the agreed time/cost ceiling, only after a known failure. Unknown download/import outcomes require receipt inspection, not blind repeat. A manual upload is an explicit fallback with source provenance; it does not prove automatic acquisition.
- **Handover evidence:** real-format CSV, preserved original, reference-only diff, reviewed ambiguous/duplicate cases, source coverage, REI preview totals and one interrupted/recovered Windows run.
- **Measure:** staff active minutes, rows reviewed, uncertain rows, rework, completed source coverage and REI-accepted outcomes. Engine time and human waiting are recorded separately.
- **Current state:** review/export and saved sign-in recovery implemented and tested; actual acquisition, customer mapping, Cua action calibration and REI acceptance remain open. Link tasks P00/P01/U02/U03/W06.

### P2 · Expected bills and levy follow-up

- **Trigger:** daily source check, expected-arrival windows and agreed due-date reminders; build the first register with Kevin.
- **Inputs:** property, issuer, bill type, recurrence/expected window, agreed source mailbox and reviewer. A due date comes from the actual invoice or a verified rule; an expected arrival is a different field.
- **Execution:** create expected occurrence → look for evidence → link received invoice → propose property/due date/amount → flag missing, duplicate or contradictory evidence → Kevin confirms → reminder/approval as agreed → retain payment-arranged state until actual payment evidence is checked in REI.
- **Recovery:** incomplete inbox coverage remains incomplete. A duplicate attachment does not create another payable. Late or corrected invoices update the existing case with review, not an automatic second bill. Missing invoice creates a follow-up with an owner and next check.
- **Handover evidence:** expected-but-missing bill, late bill, duplicate, disputed amount, unknown date and payment-arranged-but-unconfirmed cases. Establish reminder frequency/quiet hours and test the actual phone route.
- **Measure:** overdue follow-ups, unreceived expected bills, false alerts, time finding evidence and cases without a named owner.
- **Current state:** full expected-occurrence/case lifecycle and alert delivery need implementation/acceptance; the proposal is a delivery commitment within the agreed scope, not current readiness.

### P3 · Daily inbox to a work list

- **Trigger:** one agreed daily scan plus explicit on-demand review; up to two approved Gmail accounts.
- **Execution:** read the new coverage window → link messages to existing threads/items → propose category, property, owner and evidence-based due date → Kevin reviews priority → preserve his edits, dismissals and completion → create a daily list and an optional weekly summary.
- **Authority:** read access only by default. Drafting is separate from sending. Labels, archive, deletion or sending need their own agreed permission; connecting Gmail is not blanket authority.
- **Recovery:** checkpoint per account; preserve partial failure; repeated scans do not duplicate work. New replies may reopen an item only with an explained material change. An ambiguous property/owner/date stays unresolved.
- **Handover evidence:** two-account isolation, repeated scans, new replies, missing attachment, uncertain property/date, preserved human edits and incomplete mailbox coverage.
- **Measure:** triage time, duplicate tasks, unassigned items and completion/follow-up age. Link M01/M02/M03/U01/A03.

## Stage 2 · CRM acceptance scope

Configure three users, standard agreed roles, one enquiry pipeline and one property/listing custom object. Import at most 2,000 records from one agreed export after a representative sample and duplicate/mapping review. Complex address/contact clean-up, attachments/full email history, multiple legacy sources or advanced permission schemes need a revised scope.

The two included Bud workflows are (1) read selected CRM context into Bud and (2) propose and, after approval, create/update a contact, enquiry or follow-up task from a selected email. Use the Twenty API through a RealBud-owned adapter with scoped credentials and a record preview. Bind the agency and CRM workspace, store external record IDs, guard against stale changes and duplicate webhook deliveries, and read back the saved record. Do not create a generic unrestricted CRM tool to stand in for these two flows.

Twenty owns contacts, properties, enquiries and tasks. RealBud owns approvals, work orchestration and execution receipts. REI owns financial records. Declare field ownership so an old CRM sync cannot overwrite Kevin's newer edits or turn an email suggestion into a financial fact.

Acceptance includes authorised read/write, denied action, changed record between review and save, duplicate event, provider timeout with unknown result, disconnected CRM, credential revocation, export, restore, and module disable/re-enable. Pause/remove the CRM integration without damaging PM records, schedules or Hermes installation. Reinstall/update Hermes without erasing CRM configuration. Prove the selected edition's role restrictions before promising them.

Core self-hosting is available under Twenty's open-source terms; premium access features can need a paid licence. Confirm edition, access rules and modification/distribution obligations before the firm quote. Avoid a source fork unless explicitly required.
