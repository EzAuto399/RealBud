# macOS first-install QA and fixes (29–30 September 2026)

This checkpoint does not establish a signed or notarized package, Windows
behaviour, a live Bud turn on a customer account, or Auston Realty acceptance.
It records a from-scratch rehearsal of an office's first day on the Mac mini
against the live website, gateway and Modelvia, the defects it found and what
was merged. The QA office is fictional-purpose ("Auston Realty (QA)",
`office-fmjwpyw52q`, client-funded); its data stayed in local QA data and is
not in the repository.

## What ran

1. Operator invite on realbud.app → billing-owner sign-in → office terms →
   office provisioned (A$200/month AI cap, A$4/request).
2. Unsigned (ad-hoc) Mac builds from `main`, installed on a Mac with the old
   0.1.18 apps and data archived to `~/RealBud-QA-archive-2026-09-30/`.
3. First run → Connect to your office (browser approval) → automatic Bud setup.

Evidence tiers reached: source, local tests, packaged build (0.1.21, 0.1.22:
ad-hoc signature verified, `pnpm smoke:mac` passed, 69 native targets checked
against macOS 13), installed device (this Mac), live integration for linking
and provisioning only.
