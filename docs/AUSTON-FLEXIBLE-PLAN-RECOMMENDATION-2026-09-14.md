# Auston flexible service option

14 September 2026. Proposed commercial option, not selected, sent, signed, invoiced or implemented. The current main agreement still leaves the monthly price open.

## Recommendation

Use three distinct charges: attributable AI/required service cost at cost; a modest fixed monthly administration fee set after month-one measurement; and separately booked assistance. Keep the A$4,500 project, A$1,000 start payment and two included care months as currently drafted. Do not quote a guaranteed monthly total before measuring their workload.

Propose A$40/hour as an Auston founding-client rate for planned remote assistance. Sell a two-hour prepaid block for A$80, usable across requests, with actual work time recorded and unused credit carried forward or refunded on cancellation. No new block or overtime without approval. Original-scope defects and included training must not consume paid help credit. Onsite visits, emergency response, new integrations and development require a separate quote. No unlimited availability or product warranty reduction is implied.

A$50/hour would produce A$100 for two hours, only A$20 more per block. At A$40/hour, two billable hours plus one unbilled hour of preparation/admin returns A$26.67 per actual hour before overhead and tax. At A$50/hour the same workload returns A$33.33. These are arithmetic examples, not market-rate evidence or a profitability forecast. Track all time; use the discount only if the actual economics work.

## Administration fee and budget

The fee covers specifically agreed cost reporting, budget alerts and periodic connection-status checks. It does not buy unlimited repairs, monitoring, infrastructure management or guaranteed response. Agree cadence and owner for each duty. Routine updates, compatibility work and new provider breakages must be assigned explicitly in the final plan; they are not silently all included or all billable as customer-requested help. Original project defect obligations remain.

Set the administration fee after measuring recurring time, non-reimbursed costs and an adequate margin. Example only: if 30 minutes/month is required and internal time is valued at A$60/hour, the labour cost alone is A$30. A proposed A$40 fee then leaves A$10 before any other overhead. If actual duties consume more time, narrow the service or quote more; do not recover losses through hidden usage markups.

Customer bill = attributable approved service costs + agreed administration fee + any separately approved help purchases, plus applicable GST. Show provider charges, currency conversion and applicable taxes transparently; avoid duplicate charging or allocating shared-account costs arbitrarily. Verify customer direct-provider billing support before offering it as an operating option.

Illustrative month, not a quote: A$35 services + A$40 administration = A$75 before applicable GST. Purchasing one two-hour block adds A$80, making A$155 cash paid that month. An unused block balance is a customer credit, not extra completed service. Higher AI use raises the total; the low admin fee does not guarantee a sub-A$300 bill. Set a written prepaid usage budget and permission for top-ups, plus alerts and missed-work status.

## Relationship and exit

Recommend a 12-month cooperation framework with fixed admin fee and discounted support rate once agreed, monthly billing and a review before renewal. A 30-day cancellation right and no automatic fixed-term rollover keep it reasonable to accept. This is a relationship and price commitment, not guaranteed 12 months of revenue. If the user instead wants a binding minimum spend/term, model the actual subsidy and obtain a specific proportionate early-exit provision; do not invent repayment of all future AI usage or claw back the historic setup discount.

For exhausted prepaid AI funds, stop new chargeable jobs and display why. For overdue administration charges, notify and allow an agreed cure period before suspending affected managed services. Preserve customer records, export access and the already-paid local licence. Never turn a disputed support bill into undisclosed revocation of the delivered software. Any actual payment gate, notice, pause or recovery mechanism requires separate implementation and verification; this drafting turn changes no live system.

At cancellation, settle actual authorised work/costs and refund unused support/usage prepayments, accounting only for previously approved non-cancellable commitments. Export and disconnect safely. No finance-account, trust-money or automatic-send authority is created.
