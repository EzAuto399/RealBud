# Auston agreement: Australian guidance comparison

Reviewed 14 September 2026 against current official guidance. This is an AI-assisted drafting comparison, not advice or sign-off from an Australian solicitor. It assesses document wording, not implementation, insurance, facts about the client's business, or judicial enforceability. Current customer files (plain-English revision, 15 September 2026): output/pdf/Auston-Service-Agreement.pdf and Auston-Kickoff-Checklist.pdf. Agreed commercial terms retained; no new legal sign-off performed.

## Findings and changes

| Topic | Current wording and assessment | Remaining limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liability cap | Reciprocal cap: greater of A$4,500 or preceding 12 months' contract service fees, excluding third-party pass-through costs. Retains remedies and exceptions for fraud, intentional misconduct, gross negligence, privacy/confidentiality breaches, injury/death and liabilities that cannot lawfully be limited. No blanket exclusion of negligence or all losses. | A reciprocal cap is not automatically fair or enforceable. Proportionality depends on foreseeable loss, scope, bargaining and insurance. Privacy/confidentiality losses remain uncapped under this wording. Do not represent the cap as maximum personal exposure. |
| Renewal | Paid terms are 12 months, billed monthly; 45-day renewal reminder, 30-day email non-renewal, same prices unless a change is affirmatively agreed. Customer can also terminate on 30 days' notice without future-month acceleration or penalty. | This is not guaranteed annual revenue. Reminder and cancellation processes must actually work. Prominent disclosure is retained in contract and message. |
| Provider termination | Revised unrestricted supplier cancellation to reasonable operational or safety grounds with 30-day notice. Statutory immediate termination rights preserved. | Reasonableness depends on actual circumstances. Do not use the clause to avoid defects or deny statutory remedies. |
| Software access | Setup expressly is not a perpetual purchase. Operational use requires active subscription, including proprietary local software. Nonpayment suspension follows notice and a cure period for undisputed fees. Customer data and outputs remain theirs; termination includes 30-day free export. Third-party components retain their own licences. | Contract does not create a working licence gate or ownership of upstream software. Do not erase source business records or terminate third-party rights. Export must be available in practice; confirm retention/deletion and backup arrangements before live data. |

The ACCC assesses unfairness using imbalance, reasonable necessity to protect legitimate interests, detriment, transparency and the contract as a whole. Only courts finally determine unfairness. Current small-business coverage thresholds differ from the pre-2023 rules. These clauses reduce obvious drafting problems but are not a compliance certificate. [ACCC contracts](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/selling-products-and-services/contracts)

Sensis is a relevant comparison: the ACCC sought clear renewal/cancellation disclosure, reminders, and reasonable grounds for supplier termination. That supports the drafting changes above, not a claim that all annual renewals are lawful. [ACCC Sensis undertaking](https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/sensis-provides-undertaking-and-amends-contract-terms)

## Consumer rights and support

Business purchases can qualify for consumer guarantees; being a B2B sole-trader contract does not itself remove them. Services can require due care and skill, fitness for purpose and reasonable time. Contractual response targets are not resolution guarantees and cannot override non-excludable rights. Later hourly billing must not charge again for legally required remedies or unfinished contracted work. [ACCC consumer guarantees](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees)

User-selected support: first two months target one business day; afterward two business days. Remote and mutually scheduled in-person help use A$50 per commenced hour outside included work. Scope/time ceiling and travel costs require prior approval. The A$100 retainer is distinct from paid hands-on help and actual usage costs.

## Scope, teaching and data

The contract now contains a simple scope and acceptance definition derived from the original offer: Kevin's demonstrated CSV, bills and daily-summary workflows; paired sample files, traceable suggestions, unchanged protected CSV fields, unknown-item handling, independent operation, pause/takeover and recovery. Acceptance is written, not inferred from silence. Dates remain jointly scheduled rather than invented.

Teaching is outcome-based: aim for a week, each session about 4–5 hours and no more than five, with no total-hour cap; supplement until the agreed operational skills are demonstrated. Two months of AI/service usage for the defined workflows are supplier-funded with no dollar cap; abnormal/new purposes pause for agreement. These are deliberate commercial exposures, not missing mandatory legal clauses. No retrospective customer charges for included usage.

A separate generic permissions annex was replaced by a concrete short permission definition plus bilateral setup emails before live use. Real account/device names, AI providers and processing locations, backup/deletion and incident contacts cannot be inferred from a contract; operational confirmation is still required.

## GST: same registered ABN confirmed by user

User confirmed that the GST-registered business uses supplier ABN 84 992 526 369. GST registration is entity-based; multiple businesses under one ABN do not register separately. [ATO small-business GST guidance](https://smallbusiness.taxsuperandyou.gov.au/goods-and-services-tax/how-do-i-register-for-gst)

Latest user decision absorbs GST within the original customer prices: A$4,500 total (A$500 / A$3,000 / A$1,000), A$100/month and A$50 per commenced support hour, all GST-inclusive. No GST is added on top. This supersedes the earlier GST-exclusive price assumption. Liability cap base remains A$4,500 and uses GST-exclusive service fees. Third-party usage quotes must show the applicable GST treatment and total without charging GST twice on costs; confirm the accounting treatment of any genuine agency reimbursements separately. Registration is user-confirmed, not independently verified from ABN Lookup.

## Before signature / before live use

Before signature: fill supplier address and client's legal name/ABN/address/representative/email, agree dates and jurisdiction. Have an Australian solicitor assess the cap, termination/access consequences and real service risk if legal assurance is required. No invented legal review or insurance coverage claim.

Before live use: agree actual setup and permissions by email, verify source examples and privacy/provider arrangements, prove export/recovery/access controls and notification procedure. No reminders, licences, account controls, payments or messages were executed by this document revision.
