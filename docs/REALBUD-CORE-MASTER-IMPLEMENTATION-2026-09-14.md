Fix defects that prevent the required outcome. Keep status and handoff documents current without rewriting historical claims as newly verified facts.

Proceed with reversible implementation, local integration, test fixtures, migrations on copies, packaging preparation and repairs without repeatedly requesting permission. Use explicit assumptions for synthetic work. Ask only for an unresolved choice that materially changes behavior, authority, privacy or deployment and cannot be resolved from source/evidence. Do not use questions to avoid difficult implementation.

Do not contact customers, authenticate as them, enroll real devices, alter live office data, enable production routines, spend money, publish releases or deploy without the applicable authorization. Existing developer/test access is not blanket customer permission. Complete the code and safe preparation while those gates remain open.

When blocked externally, record exactly what is missing, why the particular acceptance criterion needs it, the task IDs affected, what is already implemented/proven, who or what must supply it, the ready-to-run verification procedure and the next concrete action. Continue every independent workstream. Never fabricate a pass or claim total completion because local work is exhausted.

Keep an integration-ready handoff across context limits: current branch/snapshot, file ownership, changed files, accepted contracts, task/evidence state, exact failing checks and the next executable task. Do not promise unsupervised future work.

End with:

- What actually works through the product, and at which evidence level.
- Branch, HEAD and exact reviewed snapshot; changed files and migrations.
- Exact checks run, results, failures/skips and evidence locations.
- Produced artifacts, hashes, signing state and installed-platform results.
- Coverage of all 61 tasks and all six milestones, with verified versus implemented-but-unverified distinctions.
- Remaining external gates and the next concrete action for each.
- The single next highest-priority executable action.

Do not stop at the vertical slice, backend APIs, placeholder UI, workflow import, a compiled installer or a documentation handoff. Finish the complete safely executable implementation and integration scope, while leaving genuinely unperformed acceptance gates visibly open.

Start now: inspect the checkout and 14 September inputs, establish the safe baseline and ownership, close the early gates with discriminating proof, and implement through the full dependency graph.
