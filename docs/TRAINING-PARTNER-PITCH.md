# RealBud — partner briefing for licence-training providers

One page for the person who decides what bundles with your certificate. Facts only; product status is disclosed at the bottom.

## Your graduate's first year, honestly

Newly licensed property managers inherit the interrupt layer: arrears chasers every morning, owner emails, tradie calls, portal clicks across a book they did not build. The big PMS platforms sell AI assistants (PropertyMe's AiMe, Property Tree's Alex) that smooth the happy path **inside their own system**. Most offices your graduates land in either run those platforms without the AI tier, or run something else entirely.

Nobody sells the assistant for *the rest*.

## What RealBud is

A supervised desktop assistant for an Australian residential property-management desk. It sits **on** whatever PMS the office already runs and takes routine hours off the PM:

- **Desk** — morning money check turns the arrears export into exception cards. The PM allows, edits, denies, or copies the wording into their PMS. RealBud itself has no send button; the route is removed and returns 403.
- **Ask** — one thread with one worker. "What's waiting?" or "draft Friday for Oak". Anything that must leave the building becomes a Desk card needing one approval.
- **Schedule** — named loops on the office's clock: morning arrears (built) and Friday owner letters (built): one factual weekly catch-up per property, drafted from the book, copy-only.
- **You** — hands setup, worker pin, source honesty chip (live vs demo).

Per-property options carry the shop rules: grace days, courtesy window, levy-from-rent, notify channel, spend caps, and locked never-rules that cannot be edited away.

## Why it is safe to put in front of licensees

Three rules baked into the worker, enforced in code, tested in CI:

1. **Draft only** — wording waits for a human who copies it out themselves.
2. **No notices, no trust** — no Form 11/12, NSW termination, VIC NTV, rent increases, entry notices; no trust EFT, disbursements, or recon. Past the courtesy window it escalates to a licensed person.
3. **No invented legal clock** — day counts are labelled shop reminder rules, never statutory deadlines.

Approvals are manual by force (the auto-approve routes are denied at the API), tenants never talk to a bot, and every allowed action appends to the property's own record. A principal can verify all of it in an afternoon: what it used, that it cannot send, that it did not invent a clock.

## What we ask of a training partner

1. **Pilot introductions** — one office ready to fill our eight-field pilot checklist (PMS brand, named exporter, export cadence, identity column, office OS, jurisdictions, vendor test account).
2. **Placement feedback** — which graduate tasks the desk actually absorbed, so the next loop kind is chosen by evidence.
3. Optionally, bundling RealBud seats into your post-course offering once the pilot proves out.

## What we are not

Not a PMS. Not trust-accounting software. Not legal advice. Not a tenant-facing chatbot. Not a replacement for PropertyMe, Property Tree, or Reapit — we work them.

## Status (read before promising dates)

RealBud today is a **training appliance**: fixture book, CSV import matched by address or property code, fake-portal integration, both core loops built and tested. The pilot programme converts it office-by-office; the checklist lives in `docs/PILOT-CONTRACT.md` and every field needs a name from the real agency.
