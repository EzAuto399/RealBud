This can lower setup cost; no universal saving or automatic department rollout is promised. Included Google Workspace work and the 1,500+ connection catalogue remain visible. The proposal does not name the underlying agent engine. The agreement remains a review draft with customer particulars and marked terms to complete.

7
Questions before we start
1. Can Bud do the work if we ask in ordinary words?
Yes. Ask, "Prepare today's priorities." Bud checks the agreed emails and appointments, finds relevant
files and saves a linked draft list for Kevin to review. We set up these steps and teach him how to use
them. AI can make mistakes; Kevin checks the result.
2. What are you setting up for Kevin?
Bud and the agreed Google Workspace workflows are included: morning list, bill checks, ANZ
reference help, calendar/file help, five templates, weekly summary and guide. It covers one agreed
Windows 11 computer, up to two Gmail accounts, one calendar, one Google Drive folder and its
subfolders, plus a draft folder. We agree file formats and amounts of work first.
3. Who decides what Bud can see and do?
You choose the accounts, files and tasks. Kevin decides. Bud does not send messages, move money,
sign or issue legal notices. Records stay in your existing systems. Some information may go to outside
AI services; we agree those services and access before using real office information.
4. How much teaching and help do we receive?
Eight booked hours: two one-hour sessions with Kevin and up to four hours of guided practice, followed
by four half-hour check-ins during the first month of use. The teaching week is not five full days on site.
Fixing faults in the agreed work does not use these help hours.
5. Will the list be ready before Kevin arrives?
We agree and test the days, ready-by time and what to include. The computer must be awake, online
and signed in. Bud must flag missing checks. The examples here are illustrations; your actual office still
needs testing.
6. What is included in the monthly price?
Two care months are included after acceptance, then A$299/month from month three: A$100 below our
A$399 estimate for the first 12 paid months. Includes one help hour and an initial A$75 AI/service
allowance. During the first month after handover, we review actual use and may change the future
allowance by written agreement. Without agreement, current terms stay. At the allowance, new usage
pauses unless approved. We absorb approved extra usage in both included months; later approved
excess is at cost. No retrospective increase or double billing.
7. Could it help other departments or our customer records?
Yes. Other departments reuse the RealBud foundation; quotes charge added work, not the existing
core again. CRM setup is A$1,500; care is A$149/month from its own acceptance for its first 12 paid
months, A$50 below our A$199 estimate. Combined care is A$448/month while both rates apply.
Hosting is separate. Composio lists 1,500+ services/tools; agreed Google Workspace work is included,
further connections separately quoted. Airbnb and Form 11 are excluded. Catalogue:
composio.dev/toolkits, checked 14 September 2026.
8. What if we decide to stop?
The A$1,500 deposit starts the A$4,500 project and counts toward its total. If work ends early, we
account for authorised work completed and approved commitments, and return unused prepayments
under the agreement. Care can end on 30 days notice; export and disconnection are included.
Complete the agreement before signing. AUD; GST where applicable.
