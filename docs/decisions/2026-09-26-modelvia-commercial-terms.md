# Modelvia commercial terms per office

Implementation decision, 26 September 2026, on branch `claude/modelvia-live-integration`. Backed by local tests against a stand-in shaped from Modelvia `main` 49327ba. Not deployed, and not checked against the live service.

## Decision

Modelvia refuses every request of a customer that RealBud's client pays for until an active commercial policy is in force (`customer_terms_required`). The gateway now writes that policy when an operator saves an office's AI access. It chooses the billing treatment as follows:

- **Client-funded** for companies listed in `REALBUD_MODELVIA_CLIENT_FUNDED_COMPANIES`, which are the owner's office and internal offices. RealBud absorbs the usage, so there is no markup and the receipts show `priceBasis: withheld`. This follows the owner decision in [Modelvia is the only billing source](2026-09-24-modelvia-sole-billing.md) and the live policy `realbud-owner-internal-2026-09-25`.
- **Resale** for any other office, only when `REALBUD_MODELVIA_RESALE_MARKUP_BASIS_POINTS` and `REALBUD_MODELVIA_RESALE_TERMS_REFERENCE` are both set. The routing plan (`routing/MODELVIA-FIRST-REALBUD-CLIENT.md`) makes RealBud the payer and reseller for customer offices. The markup and per-office acceptance were decided later the same day (below).
- **Nothing** otherwise. The operator's save reports `terms.state: unconfigured`, and provisioning refuses the office with `modelvia_customer_not_ready` until the treatment is decided.

A policy that is already in force is never replaced. Changing how an office is billed is a dated migration at Modelvia.

## Owner decision, 26 September 2026: offices pay AI at Modelvia's rate + 30%

Recorded on branch `claude/office-ai-billing`. Backed by local tests against a stand-in shaped from Modelvia's client-key routes (`main` 927a5c2). Not deployed, and not checked against the live service.

- **Resale is the production default.** `REALBUD_MODELVIA_RESALE_MARKUP_BASIS_POINTS=3000` and `REALBUD_MODELVIA_RESALE_TERMS_REFERENCE=realbud-office-terms-2026-09-26-ai-resale-30pct` are the documented production values (`managed-gateway/DEPLOY.md`); `deploy.sh` applies them when unset, validates them, and now requires `REALBUD_MODELVIA_CLIENT_FUNDED_COMPANIES` so the owner's own and internal offices stay client-funded (free).
- **Per-office acceptance.** An office's monthly RealBud terms may carry `aiUsage {billing: resale, markupBasisPoints, termsReference}`. When its billing owner accepts those terms through the existing care-terms acceptance flow, the ledger records `ai_resale_terms_accepted` with the office's own reference, `<termsReference>@<first 32 hex of the acceptance digest>`. Saving the office's AI access writes the Modelvia resale policy with that reference; before an acceptance at the configured markup and reference it writes nothing (`terms.state: acceptance_required`). The shared reference is no longer an acceptance by itself.
- **One monthly invoice per office.** Month close (`commercial-cli.ts close`) adds one "AI usage" line per finalized Modelvia customer invoice for the office (this month, or an earlier month since its first resale acceptance, not yet consolidated), at that invoice's exact `totalCents` and `gstCents`, referencing its `CI-…` number. The office pays the one total through RealBud's existing Square checkout. Modelvia is read with RealBud's client integration key (`REALBUD_MODELVIA_CLIENT_KEY`), read only.
- **Never double-billed.** `office_ai_consolidations` is keyed by the Modelvia invoice id and immutable; a re-run close returns the closed invoice without calling Modelvia. A Modelvia invoice that was paid, or whose checkout was started, at Modelvia is refused.
- **Not finalized: wait (the safer option).** If Modelvia shows usage for the month but no finalized invoice, the close refuses with `modelvia_invoice_not_finalized` and writes nothing. Waiting keeps each month's AI on that month's invoice and the reconciliation one-to-one; the care fee is delayed only until Modelvia's operator finalizes, which the same owner controls. `--defer-ai` is the explicit escape: care-only now, the AI invoice on the next month's invoice, recorded as `aiUsage.deferredPeriods` and `ai_usage_deferred`; a deferred month still missing blocks the next close the same way.
- **GST.** Each AI line shows Modelvia's GST for that invoice, never adjusted; the invoice GST is the inclusive GST of the one collected total (what the Square order shows). A difference of one cent sits on the care line (or a zero-amount GST rounding line); more is refused (`gst_reconciliation_required`).
- **Guards.** The close also refuses an unconsolidated Modelvia invoice dated before the office's first resale acceptance, AI still owed under terms without `aiUsage`, and Modelvia offering the office its own checkout; an office that accepted resale cannot be rebound to another Modelvia customer.
- **Owner margin view.** `GET /v1/operator/billing/margins` (website `/admin/margin`, with CSV) shows per office: AI retail, Modelvia cost (wholesale incl. Modelvia's fee), RealBud markup, care fee, total and margin. It uses Modelvia's per-customer margin report when `/v1/client/margin-report` answers, else Modelvia's client-scope analytics (`customerNetNanoAud` vs `platformNetNanoAud`).

- **Per-office markup (owner requirement, 26 September 2026, later).** Each office's terms carry its own `aiUsage.markupBasisPoints` (0–10000); `REALBUD_MODELVIA_RESALE_MARKUP_BASIS_POINTS` is only the default for new terms. An operator proposes a markup per office (CLI or operator route, audited); it takes effect only when the office accepts terms stating it. The acceptance appends a NEW Modelvia resale policy effective at Modelvia's own clock (its `Date` header), never mutating the old one, so earlier usage keeps its price. The margin view shows each office's markup.
- **Invoices the office recognises.** The office invoice names the office (registered/trading name, ABN, address, RealBud account id, Modelvia customer ID), lists Modelvia's own grouped lines (per model, request count, who used it) at their exact cents and GST with the `CI-…` reference, and links a per-request CSV. All-in by default; an office set to itemized also sees Modelvia's split. Wholesale, Modelvia's fee and RealBud's markup never appear on it.

## Still open for the owner

- Whether a customer office resold by RealBud should keep billing to RealBud's internal-cost billing account. Modelvia accepts resale on it because the payer is the client, the invoice issuer is the client and the fee is 0. It is still an accounting choice.
- After RealBud collects a consolidated invoice, record the payment against the Modelvia customer invoice (Modelvia's external payment record). Modelvia's own customer checkout for client `realbud` must stay `off`: the close refuses to consolidate otherwise (`modelvia_checkout_enabled`).
