# UX audit and design direction: 9 October 2026

RealBud 0.1.46 (`main` 6852499b). There were two passes over 108 screenshots of every desktop screen, each in two states: a brand-new empty office and the fictional Austin office running W1–W5. Claude reviewed them, and an independent Claude design reviewer did a second pass; Astra 6 Ultra was out of usage until 15 Oct. The owner's two Windows 0.1.46 screenshots were included.

**Evidence tier:** local tests (the built UI on fictional data), plus the installed device for the two Windows shots. No customer acceptance. Nothing was changed or deployed.

Files: `outputs/ux-audit-2026-10-09/`:
- `capture/INDEX.md` lists every screenshot.
- `review/claude-pass.md` is the first pass.
- `review/independent-review.md` is the second pass, with wireframes.

## Summary
1. **Setup and work contradict each other.** Jobs say "Runs automatically" and the status bar says "Next: …" before the computer is linked or any pack or source exists.
2. **Desk acts as if it owned property data, though REI is the source of truth** (6 Oct decision). "Add property", "Import CSV", "Start your office book" and a "CSV live" badge lead the screen, and nothing says "From REI · checked 07:02".
3. **The same things appear in several places.**
   - One queue is shown four times.
   - Properties and Bills each have two or three entry points.
   - Desk has four header buttons, and there are four ways to make a job.
   - Three onboarding checklists compete.
4. **The core shell is property-management only.** Properties, Bills, tenants, the money check, the owner letter and a "Hermios" tab appear for every office, which contradicts the 21 Sep decision ("keep property and REI specifics in the pack").
5. **Internal words and configuration leak to staff:**
   - "Mock maintenance comparison for Sherry", "model service", "Gmail scope"
   - "Open Agency workflow setup"
   - a connector address/token form
   - "Source-run key" (8 Oct FDE decision: staff don't see configuration)

## How data works today (owner's question)
- **RealBud does not replace REI.** Desk keeps a dated local copy of REI facts in `~/.realbud/desk.json`, encrypted. It also keeps RealBud's own records, which REI doesn't have:
  - tasks, evidence and approvals
  - bill predictions and the calendar from Gmail (W2)
  - morning mail priorities (W3)
  - supplier checks (W4)
  - inspection drafts (W5)
  - bank reference batches (W1)
- **The daily REI read is "REI morning refresh".** It runs on weekdays at 07:00 and is off until the office switches it on.
  - It reads Tenants, Arrears, Owners and tasks due in the person's signed-in REI session, read only.
  - REI wins. A Desk edit is held as "Differs from REI", and a property that is new in REI arrives as a one-click card.
  - If REI is signed out, it records "Missed: sign in to REI".
- **The only write to REI is the W1 bank receipts upload,** to REI's receipting preview after a per-file approval. A person then posts the receipts in REI.
- **Gaps:**
  - The column mapping comes from the fictional portal. It needs one real REI export.
  - Tenants are copied twice (Desk, and W1's tenant list), each refreshed separately.
  - A property removed from REI is never flagged on Desk.
  - Freshness is not shown per fact.

## Design model: core vs pack
**Core (the same for every office and department):**
- the rail
- queue states (Needs you / Next / Waiting / Done today)
- the work-item anatomy: why it's here, evidence, proposed action, Approve/Edit/Decline, recovery
- approval and recovery cards
- the locked right-panel cards
- Schedule mechanics: on/off, next run, blocked reason, last result, "Open on Desk"
- the source-freshness chip
- the 5-step setup frame
- formatters and permissions

**Pack (installed by the FDE, declarative):**
- the main noun and its record tab
- columns and filters
- each noun's source (REI / bank feed / none), which alone decides whether Add/Import/Remove appear
- up to 3 work tabs, each with one primary action
- starter jobs (off, each listing the sources it needs)
- extra right-panel cards
- setup step copy
- Bud's greeting and example asks
- pinned app tabs (e.g. Hermios)

**Rule:** Schedule says *when*. Desk is where the work is done, and every job links to its Desk tab.

```
(a) PM office, REI
DESK  [From REI · 07:00]                      [Approve next] [Ask Bud] [⋯]
Tabs: Needs you 3 | Properties | Bills | Inspections
Properties: Address | Tenant | Rent | Arrears | Source (REI / Differs)
SCHEDULE  Needs setup: Bank references – [Sign in to REI]
          On: REI refresh 07:00 · Money check 07:30 · Owner letter Fri

(b) Sales, no PMS
DESK  [Kept in RealBud]                       [Add contact] [Ask Bud] [⋯ Import]
Tabs: Needs you | Contacts | Listings | Open homes
SCHEDULE  On: Mail priorities 08:00 · Vendor report Fri

(c) Accounts
DESK  [From REI + bank feed · 08:00]          [Review 4 unmatched] [Ask Bud] [⋯]
Tabs: Needs you | Bank references | Bills | Owner statements
Bank references: stepper here (Review › REI upload › You process › Check)
```
